What is Kindle Battles / E-ReaderCodingTime: E-ReaderCodingTime, or more properly Kindle Battles, is a turn(?) based fighting simulator. 
It has a few different modes, with my personal favorite being the 3 different campaign battles. Theres a tutorial in the game, and in this read-me.
<br>
<br>
What did I learn while making Kindle Battles: I learned a lot about Javascript while coding this. I was able to code a lot faster towards the end, and bug-fix significantly faster. I also had a lot of fun
<br>
<br>
If I could redo something, what would I redo: If I had the opprotunity to redo any part of Kindle Battles, I would spend a lot less time on Multi Mode. It was a huge chunk of the time I spent, and I am overall much happier with Campaign mode, 
which took a fraction of the time to get a decent amount done. Hindsight 20/20, and Multi Mode probably contributed to me being able to do Campaign mode relatively fast as I had a base and a knowledge of how to do the code well.

Tutorial and explanation behind the thought process of many things:
<br>
Ally Attacks: Ally Attacks can be found on the bottom 5th of the screen. There will sometimes be 3 attacks for one ally, or 6 attacks for 2 allies, depending on the mode. If you click on them, they will be selected (denoted by the thicker border), and once the yellow meter above any character <br>
on the right shows up, will do that attack. The attack does not unselect after being used, so if you hit attack one and then do nothing else, you will keep using attack one
<br>
<br>
Enemy Attacks: The enemies on the left of the screen when their attack meters (the bar above their sprite) hits 0, and will enact one of their 3 attacks. You can see what this attack did based on the text to the right of the sprite
<br>
<br>
Speed Modes: Speed modes can be found in a few different places. In multi mode you can find it on the character selection screen, and in campaign mode you can find it near the start button before you hit it. There are 5 speed settings, Snail, Slow, Standard, Speedy, and Swift. It defaults to slow, which has a turn time 
of 6 seconds
<br>
<br>
Tutorial Battle Explanation: The tutorial battle turned out well (hopefully), I think it successfully shows a new player what to do.
<br>
Campaign Mode: Campaign mode is by far my favorite, I like how it turned out, and think it is more engaging than MultiMode. I am dissapointed by only being able to complete 3 levels, and not being able to do phase 2 of Level 3, but I think it still turned out well.
The first campaign level has some attack visuals, which turned out okay, not sure how I feel about them yet, but it was worth a try
<br>
<br>
MultiMode Explanation: MultiMode was the first part of Kindle Battles made, and by far had the most time spent on it, as I had very little idea as to what I was doing through most of it. There are 12 unique characters, with 6 each for the Ally and Enemy Side.
I think it turned out alright, but overall is a bit biased toward the ally side, and could have been better.
